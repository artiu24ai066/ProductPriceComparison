# AI-Powered Price Comparison Platform (PriceWise)

An AI-powered full-stack price comparison platform that helps users find the best deals across multiple e-commerce websites by scraping product data, matching similar products, predicting price trends, and providing intelligent buying recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔍 Product Data Collection
- Automated web scraping to collect product information from multiple e-commerce platforms.
- Extracts product name, price, ratings, specifications, images, and reviews.

### 🧠 Intelligent Product Matching
- Identifies identical products listed differently across different websites.
- Uses product attributes and similarity techniques for accurate matching.

### 📈 Price Prediction
- Predicts whether a product price is likely to decrease in the future.
- Helps users decide whether to buy now or wait.

### ⏰ Best Time to Buy Recommendation
- Suggests the optimal time to purchase based on historical price trends.

### 🤖 Smart Recommendations
- Provides recommendations beyond price comparison.
- Example: Suggests a slightly expensive laptop if it offers significantly better RAM, storage, or specifications.

### 💬 Review Sentiment Analysis
- Collects and analyzes customer reviews.
- Determines overall product sentiment and quality insights.

### 🛡️ Fake Discount Detection
- Detects misleading discounts by analyzing previous price trends.

### 📊 Price Trend Visualization
- Displays interactive graphs showing price changes over time.

### 🔔 Price Drop Alerts
- Sends email notifications when the price of a wishlist product drops.

### 👤 User Features
- Secure user authentication.
- Wishlist management.
- Search history tracking.
- Budget-based product search.
- AI chatbot assistance.
